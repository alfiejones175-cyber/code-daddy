Produce a decision-ready research packet before implementation when facts, compatibility, or direction are uncertain. Use only available tools and sources; this skill does not grant network, shell, file, or permission access.

## Inputs

Capture the question to resolve, product or repository context, constraints, decision owner, and the evidence needed to make the choice. List assumptions explicitly. If the assignment lacks essential context, research what can be established and send the missing questions to the lead in one consolidated group.

## Method

1. Inspect the supplied repository, documentation, schemas, tests, and existing behavior first.
2. Prefer primary sources: official specifications, vendor documentation, upstream source, original papers, standards, and project-owned records. Use secondary sources only to locate or compare primary evidence.
3. For every material claim, record the source title, direct URL or repository path, date/version if relevant, the claim it supports, and any caveat. Distinguish observed facts, source claims, and inference.
4. If considering templates, code, imagery, or other assets, trace provenance and licensing/reuse conditions. Assess fit for this project's stack, design, accessibility, security, maintenance, and intended use. Do not copy material whose terms or origin are unclear.
5. Compare viable options against the stated constraints. Include a recommendation and explain uncertainty, migration cost, and what evidence could change the recommendation.
6. When behavior spans components, show a concise Mermaid flowchart or sequence diagram. Label inferred or proposed paths; diagrams explain the model but do not prove implementation behavior.
7. Regroup findings into one set of material questions and options for the lead to present to the user before implementation.

## Output and handoff

Return: question and scope; findings with source-backed claims; fit and license notes where relevant; options and tradeoffs; recommendation; confidence and unresolved facts; one consolidated question list; and a diagram when useful. Point to inspected files or sources directly. Never claim a source was opened, a behavior was reproduced, or a check passed without evidence.
